Members’ invitation

AGM & Open Social
 

Dear Member of Chorlton Bikes,

We have the pleasure of inviting you to our first Annual General Meeting and Open Social as a Community Benefit Society.

Our members are our lifeblood – so please do come along!

When

Wednesday 25 November
6pm – 9pm

Where

Dulcimer, Wilbraham Road
Private upstairs room
Add to your calendar

We have a private upstairs room booked, snack food will be available, and the evening will have an emphasis on the personal and social!

Encouraging new members

This year we are opening the event to non-members as well as the more than a hundred of us who are paid-up members – still only £1 to join!

You will also hear about the advanced discussions we have been having with Manchester City Council about a possible local Chorlton Bikes Hub – our own premises here in Chorlton!

This is where having a vibrant, involved membership helps to build our case with the Council.

We urge all members to come – and to mention us to your friends and encourage them to come too, to discover just how great a Community Benefit Society Chorlton Bikes is!

Ways to get more involved

There will be a short business side of the event (Tom & Anna updating on our progress, Ben presenting our financial position and Claire the election of Directors) – but in the main the evening will be a chance to meet both new and established riders, find out more about our big plans for 2027, and how members can get involved.

We have a monthly get-together called the Mission Circle – a business meeting to ensure Chorlton Bikes runs smoothly, which is open to all members.

And we are actively seeking help in four key areas:
 
 Bike mechanics support, joining our more practically-minded volunteers
 
 Filing of our prepared accounts, which could help save us vital money – anyone out there with accountancy knowledge?
 
 Grant-funding applications as part of a small team
 
 Community engagement – for example growing our social media, graphic design, videography

If you can help in any of those or would like to explore becoming more involved in other ways, please come along on the night and have an informal chat with any of our current Directors or Co-ordinators – either before the main business between 6pm and 7pm, or after 8pm.

Or drop us an email at hello@chorltonbikes.coop.

Email us about getting involved

And if you are interested in becoming a Director of Chorlton Bikes you can also chat to us, either on the night or at another convenient time.

So please do put this event in your calendars, consider inviting others, come along to hear our progress – and chat about how you might get more involved.

And let’s together prepare Chorlton Bikes for yet another exciting year ahead.

Best wishes,

Abbie, Ben, Claire, Debbie, Nick and Reg

Current Directors, Chorlton Bikes
