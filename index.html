What we do

If we can do it on a bike, we'll do it.

Community work, and deliveries for local businesses and the public sector. Open a card if you might use a service, get involved, or benefit from it.

Surplus food collections

Every day, volunteers collect surplus food from local businesses and residents and deliver it to foodbanks across South Manchester. It cuts food waste and gets good food to people who need it.

If you're a shop, café or household with food to pass on, we'd like to hear from you.

Unicorn Shop & Drop

For people with mobility issues or other significant barriers getting to Unicorn Grocery. We offer Shop & Drop, or You Shop, We Drop.

To be eligible you need a physical or mental health obstacle that stops you shopping in person, and you live in Chorlton or the M21 borders.

Community trishaw rides

We run a trishaw scheme across South Manchester, so older people and anyone with limited mobility can get back out in the fresh air.

Rides are free when they're offered through community organisations.

Bikes4Refugees donations

We take unwanted bikes, revive them, and pass them on with Refugee Aid (Care UK). A donated bike can mean work, college, or simply getting across the city.

If you need a bike, contact one of the refugee agencies or charities in Manchester who may be able to help.

Bike library

With support from TfGM we run a bike library, to get more people in the community onto bikes. That includes Bromptons, a tandem for co-commuters, and an Estarli electric cargo bike families can try.

Community projects

Have an idea for a community project? Our riders and fleet of electric cargo bikes can help you make it happen.

Healthcare logistics

Our NHS contract is a daily round: collections from doctors' surgeries across South and Central Manchester, specimens carried in specialised containers to local hospitals.

We can support other NHS trusts, health institutions and pharmacies.

Food & catering deliveries

Bread from city-centre bakeries to local shops, platters to businesses, cheese and veg boxes to people's doors. Business-to-business and straight to the customer.

The bikes are fitted so food arrives dry, secure and contamination-free.

Leaflet & magazine distribution

We deliver leaflets and flyers to South Manchester residents, and magazines to businesses and social venues — bars, theatres and the like. Local knowledge is the point.

Trishaw hire

Hire the trishaw and a pilot for a wedding, a birthday, a celebration or a corporate event.

Other deliveries

Regular rounds or a one-off. We've carried Christmas trees, books, beer and plenty more. Tell us what you need.

Who we are

From lockdown, into a society.

The mission is to reduce carbon emissions, support the communities we live in, and create Real Living Wage jobs. About 80% of what we spend goes to the riders and the staff.

  1. 2020

    Lockdown

    A grassroots response to Covid. Neighbours with cargo bikes, so people in Chorlton could keep shopping locally. That was Chorlton Bike Deliveries.

  2. 2025

    A society

    We became a Community Benefit Society, and Chorlton Bikes. Member-owned, one share, one vote. Membership is a pound.

  3. Now

    South Manchester

    From the Boiler House in Moss Side: fewer emissions on local streets, support for the communities we live in, and Real Living Wage jobs.
